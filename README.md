# AI Trip Planner ✈️🌍

Welcome to the AI Trip Planner, a smart travel companion designed to take the hassle out of vacation planning. This Flutter-based application empowers you to create personalized, day-by-day itineraries with the help of AI, turning your dream trip into a well-organized reality.

## The Problem with Trip Planning

Planning a trip can be overwhelming. It often involves hours of research, sifting through countless reviews, and struggling to fit all your desired activities into a coherent schedule. This can lead to decision fatigue and take the excitement out of your upcoming adventure.

The AI Trip Planner solves this by automating the most time-consuming parts of the process, allowing you to focus on what matters most: enjoying your travels.

## Key Features ✨

-   **Dual User Modes:**
    -   **Plan Anonymously:** Jump right into planning without needing to create an account.
    -   **Sign Up / Sign In:** Create a profile to save your trips, view your travel history, and pick up where you left off.

-   **AI-Powered Destination Suggestions:**
    -   Not sure where to go? Simply select your interests (like "beach," "hiking," or "museums"), and our AI will suggest the perfect destination for you.

-   **Personalized Itinerary Generation:**
    -   The app generates a complete, day-by-day activity plan based on your chosen destination, travel dates, and interests.

-   **Interactive Activity Planning:**
    -   Each day of your trip is laid out on a card. You can view AI-suggested activities, add them to your schedule, and see your itinerary come to life.

-   **Detailed Activity Information:**
    -   Get all the essential details for each activity, including:
        -   **Description:** A summary of the activity.
        -   **Location:** City and address.
        -   **Expected Duration:** Plan your time effectively.
        -   **Estimated Cost:** Keep your budget in check.

-   **User Profiles:**
    -   Your personal travel hub where you can view all your saved trips. Each trip is displayed on an interactive card with a preview image and the trip duration.

## How It Works: Your Journey 🗺️

1.  **Start on the Landing Page:** Choose to either sign in/sign up or start planning as an anonymous user.
2.  **Select Your Interests:** Pick from a variety of activities like "Food Tours," "Nightlife," or "Historical Sites."
3.  **Choose Your Destination:** Select a city from our list or let our AI suggest one based on your interests.
4.  **Enter Your Trip Details:** Specify your travel dates and the number of adults and children.
5.  **Get Your Custom Plan:** The app will generate a day-by-day plan filled with activities tailored to you.
6.  **Customize Your Itinerary:** Browse AI-suggested activities for each day and add the ones you like to your schedule.
7.  **Save Your Trip:** Once you're happy with your plan, you can save it to your profile (requires sign-in).
8.  **View Your Saved Trips:** Access all your finalized trips from your profile, view their itineraries, and get inspired for your next adventure.

## Technical Stack 🛠️

-   **Framework:** Flutter
-   **State Management:** Flutter Bloc & Riverpod
-   **Networking:** Dio
-   **Dependency Injection:** GetIt
-   **Storage:** Flutter Secure Storage for safely storing authentication tokens.
